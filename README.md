Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2 USDC).

`main.py` is deterministic and uses no network: it writes a two-line warning to your past self to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Don't delete the voicemail your grandmother left on your birthday before you listen to it twice.
> Her voice was the only recording of her laugh you would ever own, and you spent years wishing you could hear it once more.
